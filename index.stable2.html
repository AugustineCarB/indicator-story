How we built our
TBL Liquidity Indicator

A short walk through how the indicator was constructed, from the raw index to the buy and sell signals.

Calculate the change within that 30-day period.

Roll that 30-day change throughout the life of our index, and you get a cycle.

Rolling 30-day change of our index

The raw 30-day change is noisy, so we smoothed it out.

This smoothed cycle moves closely with 30-day changes in risk assets like the S&P 500.

Thus, finding peaks and troughs in this cycle identifies liquidity headwinds or tailwinds.

These peaks and troughs are found where the slope of the cycle is flat.

TBL Liquidity Cycle
S&P 500 30-day change

We thus chart the slope of the cycle to find the spots where the slope is zero.

When the slope crosses below zero, the cycle has turned negative, and when the slope crosses above zero, the cycle has turned positive.
